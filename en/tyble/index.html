Trace Your BLE

BLE connection and data transfer assistant

  • iOS
App Store

Trace Your BLE is a Bluetooth Low Energy helper for developers. Scan for nearby BLE devices, inspect their services, send and receive commands with a specific device, and keep your data synced and backed up with iCloud.

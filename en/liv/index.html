Animal Crossing Daily is a small companion for Animal Crossing: New Horizons players. Note each villager's catchphrase, greeting and the nickname they gave you, along with birthdays, special days, K.K. concerts and other good times on the island.

What Animal Crossing Daily can do

Every move-in and move-out, kept in Villager Footprints

Met on a mystery tour, invited from a friend's island, won over at the campsite, summoned with an amiibo — or the day they decided to leave on their own. Each villager's arrival and departure is recorded automatically, with the date and how it happened.

Over time, it becomes the story of who has lived on your island.

All 413 villagers at your fingertips

Birthday, star sign, personality, species, hobby, catchphrase, saying and favorite song — everything about a villager on one page, with villager names in English, Japanese and Chinese.

Filter by favorites, residents, amiibo, photos, posters, campsite, mystery tour, gender, species and personality, and mark what you've collected on each villager's page.

Remember what villagers say to you

Note new greetings, new catchphrases and the nicknames villagers come up with for you, each with its date.

Heart the nicknames you love. Each villager's page also gathers your greeting, their new catchphrase and their nickname idea for you.

Island life, one entry at a time

Log K.K. concerts with the date, the villagers who came and the song — and play your favorite songs. Keep track of who threw your birthday party each year, who you met on mystery tours, and whether you invited them to move in.

Campsite visitors, and the photos and posters you've collected, are all kept too.

Works with your iPhone

Add a villager's birthday to your iPhone calendar with one tap, so you never miss the day.

Your records sync between your iPhone and iPad through iCloud, and you can search for villagers in Spotlight.
